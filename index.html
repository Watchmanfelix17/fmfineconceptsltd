FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with FM Fine Concept Ltd.

Where do you offer your construction services?

We are headquartered in Karu, Abuja, but we are fully equipped to handle and supervise building projects for clients nationwide across Nigeria.

How much does a building or renovation project cost?

Every project is unique, and pricing depends on the scope, materials, and timeline. We prefer to discuss your specific needs first. Tap any WhatsApp button on this site to chat with us, and we can provide a tailored estimate.

Do you handle both structural building and finishing?

Yes. We provide end-to-end services. From the initial groundbreaking and block work to the final wall screeding, painting, and interior finishing, we supervise every step to ensure exceptional quality.

Do you provide project supervision for existing builds?

Absolutely. If you already have a project underway but need an expert on-site to ensure structural integrity and timeline management, we offer dedicated project supervision services.
